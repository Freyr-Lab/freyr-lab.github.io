Privacy Policy

Freyr Design LTD  ·  Last updated: May 4, 2026

Freyr Lab is a personal training and activity tracking app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights over it. We will never sell your data or share it with third parties without your explicit consent.

1. Who We Are

Freyr Lab is operated by Freyr Design LTD, based in Washington, USA. Questions about this policy can be directed to privacy@freyrdesign.com.

2. What We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the app's core functionality:

  • Account information — email address and password (stored securely via Supabase Auth).
  • Activity and fitness data — workouts you log or import, including type, duration, distance, heart rate, GPS route, pace, steps, and related metrics.
  • Health data from Apple HealthKit — only data you explicitly authorize during onboarding. We read workout history and relevant health metrics for import purposes. We do not write to HealthKit without your action.
  • Calendar data — if you connect Google Calendar, we read event data to assist with training plan scheduling. We do not modify your calendar without your action.
  • Device and usage data — crash reports and error logs collected via Sentry to help us identify and fix bugs. These reports include device type, OS version, app version, and a stack trace. They do not include your fitness or health data.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and sync your activity history across your devices.
  • To generate training insights, plans, and recommendations within the app.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability.
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on servers provided by Supabase (PostgreSQL database) hosted in the United States. Data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Data at rest is encrypted by the database host. Auth tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.

We apply row-level security at the database layer so that each user can only access their own data.

5. Third-Party Services

We use a small number of third-party services to operate the app:

  • Supabase — database and authentication infrastructure. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry — crash reporting and error monitoring. Crash reports do not contain your fitness or health data. Sentry Privacy Policy.
  • Google Calendar API — only accessed if you choose to connect your calendar. Data is used only to assist with scheduling and is not stored beyond what is needed for that purpose.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to any third party, and we do not allow any third party to access your data without your explicit consent.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is soft-deleted immediately and permanently purged within 30 days. You can request immediate permanent deletion at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Opt out of any future sale of your data (we do not sell data).

Washington residents should be aware that the Washington My Health MY Data Act (MHMDA) provides additional protections for health-related data. We collect health and fitness data solely to operate the app on your behalf and do not share it with third parties without your consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@freyrdesign.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Freyr Lab is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If changes are material, we will notify active users via email or an in-app notice.
